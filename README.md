One short, funny rant for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046): the self-checkout machine that accuses you of shoplifting the groceries you just scanned yourself.

> The self-checkout yelled 'unexpected item in the bagging area' at a bag of apples I scanned myself, like I'd smuggled them in under my coat. I came in for milk. Now six strangers watch me re-scan my own groceries while a teenager in a vest sighs like I committed a crime. I left with milk, a criminal record, and trust issues.

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only, deterministic) writes that rant to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (326 characters, no trailing newline).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
